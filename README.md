# Parking Slot Detection System

## 🚗 Overview

**Parking Slot Detection System** is an intelligent parking management solution designed to make parking **easier, faster, and more convenient**, especially in **hill stations, tourist destinations, crowded cities, shopping areas, and other high-traffic locations**.

The system uses **computer vision and AI-based image processing** to detect available and occupied parking slots in real time. Instead of drivers spending valuable time searching for a parking space, the system helps them quickly identify available slots and reduce unnecessary traffic and congestion.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Finding a parking space in crowded places and hill stations can be difficult due to:

* Limited parking space
* Heavy tourist traffic
* Narrow roads and difficult terrain
* Drivers spending too much time searching for parking
* Increased traffic congestion
* Fuel wastage caused by unnecessary driving
* Poor visibility of available parking slots

## 💡 Proposed Solution

The **Parking Slot Detection System** automatically analyzes images or video from cameras installed in parking areas and identifies whether each parking slot is **occupied or available**.

The system can display the parking status to users, allowing them to locate an available space quickly and improving the overall parking experience.

## ✨ Key Features

* 🔍 **Real-Time Parking Detection** – Detects occupied and vacant parking slots.
* 🅿️ **Available Slot Identification** – Helps drivers locate free parking spaces.
* 📷 **Camera-Based Monitoring** – Uses cameras to monitor parking areas.
* 🤖 **AI/Computer Vision** – Automatically analyzes parking areas.
* 🚦 **Traffic Reduction** – Reduces vehicles circulating while searching for parking.
* ⛰️ **Hill Station Friendly** – Useful in tourist destinations with limited parking facilities.
* 🏙️ **Crowded Area Support** – Suitable for malls, markets, railway stations, hospitals, tourist spots, and city centers.
* ⏱️ **Time Saving** – Reduces the time required to find parking.
* ⛽ **Fuel Saving** – Less unnecessary driving means lower fuel consumption.
